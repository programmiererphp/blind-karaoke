## Findings

### BLOCKER BK-D1-01 — Blind matching does not progress
**Observed:** Both complete compatible profiles can be filled and “Match suchen” can be clicked, but each browser remains on “Deine Sucheinstellungen”. No Match proposal appears.

**Expected:** The second compatible search must produce one shared Match record/proposal, and both users must be able to continue to independent confirmation.

**Impact:** Blocks the main product flow and prevents verification of:
- mutual confirmation
- identity/contact reveal
- automatic shared Karaoke Room creation
- realtime song queue
- Karaoke launch
- Duet Roulette inside a real matched room
- private after-karaoke feedback
- mutual Karaoke Friend creation

### HIGH BK-D1-02 — Missing explicit no-match/search state
**Observed:** After a user searches before another compatible candidate exists, the UI simply remains on the preferences form.

**Expected:** Show SEARCHING while the request is in progress, then either MATCH_PROPOSED or the specified honest NO_MATCH state with Retry / Change preferences / Sing with friends.

**Impact:** Users cannot tell whether search ran, failed, or is still running.

### HIGH BK-D1-03 — “Mit Freunden singen” does nothing visible
**Observed:** Clicking the Home CTA succeeds as a click event, but the rendered body/UI remains identical. No room, invite code, link or join state is shown.

**Expected:** Create a two-person Friends Room and immediately show/share its invite code/link.

### MEDIUM BK-D1-04 — Form fields lack programmatic labels
**Observed:** Visible text such as “Nickname”, “Alter”, “Stadt” is not programmatically connected to the corresponding form controls. Playwright `getByLabel()` cannot locate Nickname or Alter.

**Expected:** Every form control has an associated `<label for>` + `id`, or an equivalent accessible name.

**Impact:** Accessibility and robust keyboard/screen-reader/test interaction are reduced.

### MEDIUM BK-D1-05 — Fixed bottom navigation overlaps footer/legal area
**Observed:** On mobile and desktop screenshots the fixed bottom navigation visually covers/crowds the footer; “Impressum” / “Datenschutz” sit immediately behind/under the nav area.

**Expected:** Content/footer has enough bottom safe-area/padding so no content is obscured by the fixed nav.

### LOW BK-D1-06 — Desktop remains a narrow mobile column
**Observed:** At 1440 px the app remains approximately phone-width in the center with very large empty side gutters.

**Expected:** Preserve the mobile-first design but use the Delivery 1 responsive desktop rule: a sensible wider centered layout without changing the product flow.
