# Blind Karaoke — Product Concept

## Purpose
Blind Karaoke helps two people meet through one concrete real-world activity: singing karaoke together.

Instead of browsing a large public profile catalogue, the app tries to find **one suitable duo partner** and gives the pair a simple path from match to a shared karaoke experience.

## Primary users
Adults who want to meet a new person locally and prefer a concrete shared activity over profile browsing or prolonged messaging.

## Core differentiator
**One blind duo match, then action.**

The product should feel like a karaoke/social activity app rather than a conventional dating feed.

## Central product journey
1. User states practical and musical preferences.
2. App finds one mutually compatible local duo.
3. Both independently confirm.
4. The duo coordinates a real meetup.
5. They use a shared Karaoke Room and song queue.
6. Afterward both privately decide whether they want to sing together again.
7. Mutual interest creates a reusable Karaoke-Friend connection.

## Product principles
- real-world interaction over endless browsing
- duo-first rather than group-first
- minimal profile exposure
- explicit mutual preferences
- music compatibility is useful but not a hard barrier
- simple mobile-first flows
- realtime synchronization where two people interact
- no unnecessary AI dependency for deterministic product logic
- no dead/mock buttons in accepted Deliveries
- real deployed Browser behavior is the QA truth

## Major non-goals for the early product
- public profile directory
- swipe feed
- social feed
- complex in-app messaging
- follower/like mechanics
- GPS tracking
- proprietary lyrics/audio hosting
- voice scoring/pitch analysis
- groups larger than two

## Long-term direction
Turn the initial Blind Karaoke match into a durable lightweight social connection:

**match → coordinate → meet → sing → reconnect**

Future Deliveries may improve meetup coordination, shared karaoke preparation and repeat meetups while preserving the simple duo-first product identity.
