# Design Asset Provenance

The accepted visual direction for Blind Karaoke was developed through concept mockups in ChatGPT.

## Accepted direction
Primary source concept GenID:

`39051671-d178-4a15-ab4b-81e526020a03`

Characteristics:
- predominantly white background
- light gray section surfaces
- restrained pink primary actions
- restrained violet secondary accents
- large, clear mobile controls
- no dark/esoteric/neon-heavy theme
- Blind Karaoke is the dominant product feature

## Repository graphics
The `design/*.svg` files are repository-native vector implementation references derived from the accepted direction and current written specification. They intentionally contain no `wfwebapp` branding.

## Excluded concepts
Earlier dark or neon-heavy drafts and any mockups visibly branded `wfwebapp` are not product references and should not be used for implementation.

## Deployed screenshots
Actual screenshots of the running webapp do not exist until implementation. After deployment, store verified screenshots in `design/screenshots/` and reference them from the browser QA report.
